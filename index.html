About

Editing for people who live the story before they film it.

I specialize in van-life, camper build, travel, and documentary-style videos. My focus is turning raw footage into stories that feel natural, cinematic, and authentic — never over-produced, never generic.

That means careful pacing, considered B-roll, natural sound woven with music, and a color grade that feels consistent without feeling artificial. I work closely with van builders, van-life creators, travelers, and outdoor channels who want their videos to feel more like a film than a vlog.
